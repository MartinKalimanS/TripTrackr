Support

Write to __CONTACT_EMAIL__. Please tell us the app version (You, then About), your phone model and what you expected to happen. Never send your password; we will never ask for it.

I did not get the verification email

Check your spam folder, then request a new link from the app. The link opens TripTrackr on the phone where it is installed. If it opens this website instead, tap Open the app.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. The email we send links to this website, which hands you over to the app. The link works once and expires after a short time.

Delete my account

You can do it yourself, any time, from inside the app. See how to delete your account.

Subscriptions and refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Manage or cancel a subscription in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings; refunds are decided by the store.
